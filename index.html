Welcome to ReFOUND

Find What Matters.

A smarter way to reconnect lost belongings with their owners.

ReFOUND is a campus lost-and-found platform designed to help the CVA community report missing belongings and share information about items found on campus. Instead of wondering where your belongings went, you can use the platform to help reunite them with the people who own them.

What Can You Do with ReFOUND?

🔎

Report Lost Items

Share information about an item you lost so others can help you find it.

📦

Report Found Items

Report an item you found to help its rightful owner recover it.

🤝

Reconnect Owners

Help students connect with people who may have found their missing belongings.

🏫

Campus Community

Encourage honesty, responsibility, and cooperation throughout the school.

How Does It Work?

1
Open ReFOUND

Visit the platform to access its available features.

2
Log In or Create an Account

Access your existing account or create a new account through the ReFOUND application.

3
Submit a Report

Provide the necessary information about your lost or found item using the app's features.

4
Help Items Find Their Owners

Use the available reporting process to help belongings return to their rightful owners.
